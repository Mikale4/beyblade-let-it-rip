# Beyblade: Let It Rip!

An unofficial fan game: a voxel Beyblade adventure with a walkable arcade, a story map, 3D battles, a showcase of your
collected Beyblades and Kenny's workshop for building your own.

**Play:** https://mikale4.github.io/beyblade-let-it-rip/ (phone or computer; touch and keyboard both work).

The soundtrack isn't included here, so the game plays with sound effects only. Progress is saved in your browser, so
each device keeps its own save.
